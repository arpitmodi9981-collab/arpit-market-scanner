Learning Desk

Support & resistance

A support/resistance area is a zone where price previously paused or reacted. It is not a guaranteed turning point.

Breakout vs wick

A wick crossing a level and a candle closing beyond it are different observations. Neither proves what happens next.

False breakout

Price can move beyond a level and return. Study the full sequence rather than treating one candle as certainty.

Volatility

Compare candle ranges over a consistent sample. Large candles can mean increased uncertainty, not certainty of direction.

Risk awareness

Markets can move unpredictably. Avoid leverage and decisions based on a single indicator or sample chart.

Data integrity

Always check source, timestamp, delay and licensing before interpreting market data.
