What this tool does

Displays candles, marks PDH/PDL, and describes whether a candle's wick crossed a level, where it closed, or whether price later revisited the level area. It does not predict direction or provide trend, buy/sell, CALL/PUT, entry, target, stop-loss, broker or order-execution features.
